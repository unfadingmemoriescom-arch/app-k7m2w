      How to use it
      
    
      Tap the right side of the screen for the next line, the left side to go back. A Bluetooth page-turner or keyboard works too (arrow keys, space, Page Up / Page Down).
      
    
      Gold lines are a build. The last line of a build says Land it. A short blue mark above the line means a new paragraph.
      
    
      Listen turns the microphone on. In this version it only shows what it hears, so you can check the phone understands you. Following by voice comes in the next step.
      
    
      Your place and text size are remembered on this phone. The screen stays awake while the page is open.
